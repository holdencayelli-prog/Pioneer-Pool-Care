What we do

Everything your pool needs, on one route.

From weekly upkeep to season openings, we keep Charlotte backyards swim-ready all year.

Weekly cleaning

Skim, brush, vacuum, and empty baskets — a thorough visit every week so debris never gets ahead of you.

Chemical balancing

We test and adjust chlorine, pH, and alkalinity on every visit so the water stays safe, clear, and gentle on skin.

Equipment checks

Pumps, filters, and skimmers inspected each week. We flag small issues before they become expensive repairs.

Openings & closings

Seasonal opening and winterizing done right — so every spring starts clear instead of green.

Green-to-clean

Neglected or storm-hit pool? We'll bring an algae-green mess back to swimmable, fast.

One-time service

Hosting a party or selling your home? Book a single deep-clean visit with no ongoing commitment.

Who we are

Two Gamecocks, one standard.

Pioneer Pool Care was founded by Nolan Mize and Holden Cayelli — University of South Carolina graduates and college roommates who turned a shared work ethic into a business built on showing up. After years of talking about building something together, they chose the most hands-on trade they could find: keeping Carolina backyards crystal clear.

Nolan runs day-to-day operations in the Charlotte area, while Holden brings an engineering background to route planning and water chemistry. As owners, they answer their own phone.
